## P4 · AEM 的天花板 ｜ 0:55

**PPT 上**
- 卡片：`The four steps` → `One site: H2O -> *OH -> *O -> *OOH -> O2.`
- 卡片：`Why it is capped` → `*OH and *OOH are locked together by a ~3.2 eV scaling gap.`
- 结论：`Ideal steps are equal; real steps never are.` / `Floor ~ 0.37 V: AEM cannot go below it.`

**口述台词**

- `AEM can be improved, but it can never go below that floor.`

## P5 · LOM 的代价 ｜ 0:55

**PPT 上**
- 卡片：`When it switches on` → `When O 2p rises to E_F, lattice O leaves as O2.`
- 卡片：`What is left behind` → `Vacancy left behind -> the metal dissolves in acid.`
- 结论：`Mott-Hubbard: the O 2p band rises towards E_F.` / `LOM buys activity with the surface.`

**口述台词**
- `It switches on once the non-bonding oxygen 2p states rise above the metal–oxygen band and approach the Fermi level.`
- `The vacancy stays, and the metal is released together with the oxygen — this is the dissolution channel we must avoid in acid.`

**指图**：指能带图里的 O 2p 与 E_F → 指右侧红卡。

## P7 · 元素：为什么是钌 ｜ 1:00

**PPT 上**
- 卡片：`Why Ru and not Ir` → `More active than IrO2, far cheaper, more abundant.`
- 卡片：`Failure channel 1 — over-oxidation` → `Ru above +4 becomes soluble RuO4 and leaves.`
- 卡片：`Failure channel 2 — lattice oxygen` → `Lattice O leaves -> vacancy -> metal follows.`
- 结论：`Both channels act together: stability is proved by dissolution numbers.`

**口述台词**
- `RuO2 is more active than IrO2, ruthenium is far cheaper and more abundant, and iridium supply is the ceiling for PEM deployment.`
- `Ruthenium is oxidised beyond +4 into soluble RuO4 and leaves the electrode.`
- `Lattice oxygen participates, a vacancy is left behind, and the metal follows the oxygen out.`
- `The metal redox and the oxygen redox must be controlled together, and the proof of stabilisation has to be a dissolution measurement.`

**指图**：指左侧能带/电子转移图 → 依次指三张卡。
