# 9.24 汇报 · 讲稿骨架（关键词版）——「讲」而不是「读」

> 用途：**对着 PPT 讲**，不是念稿。每页只给四样东西：
> ① **要讲的意思**（中文，给自己听）｜② **必须说出口的英文关键句**（3–5 句，短句，背下来）｜③ **指图动作与衔接句**｜④ **忘词兜底 + 20 秒压缩版**
> 全篇目标 **9–10 分钟**（按 120 词/分钟的量），语速偏慢就按 §压缩梯 删句。
> 文体：严谨汇报，陈述句为主；**不要出现设问、比喻、口语转折**（`The catch is…`、`You know…` 这类一律不用）。

---

## 〇、讲的三条原则（开场前默念）

1. **只看关键词，不看句子**。每页记住 3–5 个词（如 `two channels`、`0.37 volts`、`go / no-go`），话由词生。
2. **一句话一停**。每说完一句停半秒，再指图或翻页——停顿比语速更像"在讲"。
3. **数字要慢，结论要停**。所有数字（1.23、0.37、6.3、839、161）单独念清楚，念完停一下再说下一句。

**通用衔接句（轮换用，避免每次都说 "and then"）**
- `That leads to the next point.`
- `This is the background; now the literature.`
- `With that in mind, here is the hypothesis.`
- `The next slide gives the plan.`
- `That is the reason for the experiment on the next slide.`

**忘词兜底（三句，语气要稳）**
- `Let me put that in one sentence: …`（然后只说那一句结论）
- `The key number here is …`（把注意力引到数字上，争取时间）
- `I will come back to the detail; the point is …`

## P3 · 背景一：瓶颈是耐久性 ｜ 0:55

**要讲的意思**：PEM 与可再生能源匹配（三个要素）→ 阳极是约 1 M 质子（pH 0）→ 只有 Ir、Ru 氧化物稳定 → 铱稀缺、连 IrO₂ 都溶 → 所以约束是"低载量下的耐久性"。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `PEM water electrolysis matches intermittent renewables: high current density, fast response, high-purity hydrogen.`
2. `The anode runs at about one molar proton activity, pH zero. Almost no transition-metal oxide is stable there; in practice only iridium and ruthenium oxides are.`
3. `Iridium is one of the scarcest elements, and even its oxide dissolves as [IrO4]2- at high potential.`
4. `So the constraint is durability at low noble-metal loading, not activity.`

**指图**：说第 2 句时指右侧电解槽示意图，强调阳极侧。
**衔接**：`That constraint is the reason this project uses ruthenium.`
**忘词兜底**：指着图说 `The anode is the acidic side; only Ir and Ru oxides survive there.`
**压缩版**：1 + 4 两句（PEM 匹配 + 约束结论）。

---

## P4 · 背景二：Ru 的悖论 ｜ 0:50

**要讲的意思**：选钌两个理由 → 两条失效通道 → 一个设计权衡 → 目标是稳定化，且要用溶解指标证明。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `RuO2 is more active than IrO2 for oxygen evolution, and ruthenium is cheaper and more abundant.`
2. `Ruthenium fails through two channels.`
3. `The first is over-oxidation: it is oxidised beyond +4 to soluble RuO4. The second is lattice-oxygen participation: lattice oxygen is consumed, and the metal is released with it.`
4. `These channels are linked to a design trade-off: a higher O 2p band centre raises activity but lowers stability.`
5. `So the objective is stabilisation, and the proof has to come from a dissolution measurement, not an overpotential.`

**指图**：说第 3 句时指左框（两个橙色框）；说第 4 句时指下方蓝框。
**衔接**：`The next slide gives the design rules from the review.`
**忘词兜底**：直接念左框里的英文短语 `over-oxidation` / `lattice-oxygen participation`，再用中文补一句意思。
**压缩版**：2 + 3 + 5（三条失效 + 结论）。

---

## P5 · 文献一：设计原则 ｜ 1:10

**要讲的意思**：四步台阶理想是 1.23 → 标度关系锁死 3.2 eV → 最大台阶 1.60 → **0.37 V 上限**；火山图；质子受体路线把决速步降到 1.49/1.33（0.26/0.10 V）；两条支持锰的结果（8000 h、eg=1）；但必须先定窗口。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `The left figure shows the four steps of oxygen evolution.`
2. `If every step were 1.23 electronvolts, the overpotential would be zero. Real surfaces are limited by the scaling relation: *OH and *OOH differ by about 3.2 electronvolts.`
3. `The largest step here is 1.60 electronvolts, which is 0.37 volts above 1.23. That is the scaling limit.`
4. `The right figure shows the volcano relation, and the proton-acceptor route: nickel or cobalt in RuO2 lowers the determining step to 0.26 or 0.10 volts.`
5. `Two results support manganese: gamma-MnO2 has been reported for 8,000 hours at pH 2, and Mn3+ gives the optimal eg occupancy, eg equal to one.`
6. `But the review adds one condition: a suitable potential window must be identified.`

**指图**：2→左图台阶段；3→点最大台阶；4→右图火山顶；6→回到左侧要点行。
**衔接**：`That condition is the background of the risk I will show later.`
**忘词兜底**：`The number to remember is 0.37 volts, the scaling limit.`
**压缩版**：1 + 3 + 5 + 6（删掉火山图与质子受体细节）。

---

## P6 · 文献二：重构化学 ｜ 1:05

**要讲的意思**：沉积的膜不是催化剂，活化后变成 MOOH 才是活性相 → 六因素 → 活化程序是变量（CV 完全 / CP 部分 / ≥8 h）→ 酸性中 Ru 的两条稳定化路线 → 先例 161 mV、>200 h。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `The deposited film is not the catalyst. During activation the surface converts to an oxyhydroxide, MOOH, which is the active phase.`
2. `The review describes four reconstruction mechanisms and six factors that control the activity — edge facets, vacancies, adsorbed anions, strain.`
3. `Two points matter for the plan. First, the activation protocol is a variable: cyclic voltammetry gives complete oxidation, chronopotentiometry only partial, and full reconstruction can take eight hours.`
4. `Second, in acid ruthenium fails through RuO4 and lattice oxygen, and two stabilisation routes are established: single-atom confinement, and higher oxygen-vacancy formation energy.`
5. `The review also records a precedent: ruthenium on alpha-MnO2 by cation exchange — 161 millivolts, over 200 hours.`

**指图**：1→左侧 pre-catalyst → MOOH 箭头；2→下方六因素方块；5→图内先例那条。
**衔接**：`That precedent is the reason for the hypothesis two slides later.`
**必守纪律**：提先例时必须加一句 `as recorded in our group's review`（这是他人工作）。
**压缩版**：1 + 3 + 5。

---

## P7 · 文献三：三条教训 ｜ 1:05

**要讲的意思**：每篇一条——局部环境决定稳定（局部 pH≈14、>200 h、98% 选择性）；掺杂 + 必须做器件（268 mV、120 h、<4.9 mV h⁻¹）；保护单元可以是变价的 → 缺口不在化学，而在"如何可控构建界面"。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `Three results from the remaining papers.`
2. `First, the local environment controls stability: a hard-Lewis-acid layer keeps the local pH near 14 at 1.6 volts — over 200 hours in seawater, about 98 per cent oxygen selectivity.`
3. `Second, doping stabilises the metal, and the work is not complete until it is tested in a device: 268 millivolts, and 120 hours with a decay below 4.9 millivolts per hour.`
4. `Third, a protecting unit can be redox-active: a variable-valence second metal buffers the oxidative charge.`
5. `So the gap is not the chemistry. It is how to build Ru-O-Mn controllably and at a larger scale.`

**指图**：三条各指一块卡片；第 5 句指底部结论带。
**衔接**：`That gap defines the hypothesis.`
**压缩版**：1 + 2 + 5。

## P8 · 缺口与假设 ｜ 1:10

**要讲的意思**：Route A 会失效（壳先溶）→ Route B 把 Ru 放进骨架共享氧 → 材料假设（MnO₂ 既载体又氧化还原介质，让 Ru 停在 +4）→ 工艺假设（沉积参数 → 耦合密度可扫描）→ 工艺变量（宿主 7 mA cm⁻² × 15 min ≈ 6.3 C cm⁻²、±2×；电流、浴 pH；Ru 三条路线）→ 文献没有这种工艺控制。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `Route A is a MnO2 shell around a RuO2 core: the shell dissolves first, and the coating fails.`
2. `Route B puts ruthenium inside the MnO2 framework, so the two share oxygen and form Ru-O-Mn.`
3. `The material hypothesis: electrodeposited MnO2 is both support and redox mediator — the Mn3+/Mn4+ couple and surface hydroxyls absorb oxidative charge and hold ruthenium near +4.`
4. `The process hypothesis: deposition parameters control phase, morphology and edge-site density, so the density and configuration of the coupling can be scanned.`
5. `The host is deposited at 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes — about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre — with a two-fold gradient; current and bath pH are variables; ruthenium comes in by co-deposition, impregnation or cation exchange.`
6. `This process control is not available in the literature.`

**指图**：1→红卡；2→绿卡；3→绿卡下方注释；5→左侧卡片两行。
**衔接**：`The main risk in this design is the manganese itself.`
**忘词兜底**：只说 `support and redox mediator` + `keep ruthenium near +4` 两句。
**压缩版**：2 + 3 + 6。

## P9 · 风险与 go / no-go ｜ 1:25

**要讲的意思**：风险是锰不是钌 → 三个电位区间（<1.4 溶、1.4–1.6 稳、>1.6 高锰酸根）→ 与 OER（>1.23 V）重叠小 → 前三周做判定实验：电位阶跃扫描、分段 ICP-MS（Ru/Mn 分开）、原位拉曼 839 cm⁻¹、四个对照 → 判定规则。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `The largest risk is manganese, not ruthenium.`
2. `Below about 1.4 volts manganese dissolves as Mn2+; between 1.4 and 1.6 volts the dioxide is stable; above about 1.6 volts soluble permanganate can form.`
3. `Oxygen evolution needs more than 1.23 volts, so the overlap is small, and the review states that this window must be identified.`
4. `The first experiment is a decision test in weeks one to three. A potential-step dissolution scan from 1.40 to 1.90 volts against RHE, each step collected separately.`
5. `Segmented ICP-MS, with ruthenium and manganese measured separately; in-situ Raman for the permanganate band near 839 reciprocal centimetres; and four controls — MnO2 only, RuO2, impregnated, substituted.`
6. `The decision rule: if manganese dissolves much faster than ruthenium and cannot be suppressed, change the MnO2 phase or the oxygen coordination, or introduce ruthenium first. Otherwise we go to interface engineering and the parameter sweep.`

**指图**：2→左侧稳定窗口图沿横轴指；4/5→右侧四条卡片；6→琥珀色 Decision rule 条。
**收尾加分句（一定要说）**：`My criterion is quantitative: if the manganese rate is five times the ruthenium rate with no passivation trend, or if the permanganate band appears, it is a no-go.` 之后加 `So I will have a binary answer for you in three weeks.`
**压缩版**：1 + 2 + 4 + 6（删 3、5 的细目）。

## P10 · 计划与时间线 ｜ 1:05

**要讲的意思**：四块——合成 / 表征 / 测试 / 器件，最后三个日期。

**必须说出口的英文**
1. `Synthesis: MnO2 on titanium mesh or felt at 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes, about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre; current and bath pH are variables; ruthenium at a near-single-atom level; one fixed activation protocol.`
2. `Characterisation: paired XRD, Raman, XPS and HRTEM-EDS before and after activation; operando XAS at the ruthenium and manganese K-edges, decisive for Ru-O-Mn; EPR for oxygen vacancies.`
3. `Testing: iR-corrected LSV for the overpotential at 10 milliamps per square centimetre, Tafel slope and EIS, normalised by mass and by ECSA; long-term chronopotentiometry with segmented ICP-MS and a stability number.`
4. `Device: one single-cell test, reported as current density, duration and retention, with Faradaic efficiency.`
5. `Milestones: laboratory entry on 8 October 2026, mid-term review on 6 January 2027, final report in April 2027.`

**指图**：只指四个标签块（Synthesis / Characterisation / Testing / Device），**不要逐行念**。
**衔接**：说完第 5 句直接进收尾句。
**压缩版**：1 + 4 + 5。
