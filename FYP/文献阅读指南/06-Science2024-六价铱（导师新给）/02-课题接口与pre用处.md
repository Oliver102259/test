## 三、可直接说的英文（3 句，背下来）

1. *"The mentor-provided precedent (Li et al., Science 2024, 384, 666–670) shows that electrodeposited MnO₂ can serve as a redox reagent to control a noble metal's oxidation state in acid: it oxidises Ir(IV) to Ir(VI) while substituting chloride ligands with oxygen."*
2. *"The structural evidence is what matters for us: EXAFS shows an Ir–O–Mn coordination at 2.92 Å, and the Ir–Mn distance is closer to the Mn–Mn distance in MnO₂ (2.88 Å) than to Ir–Ir in rutile IrO₂ (3.16 Å) — the noble metal substitutes into the MnO₂ lattice."*
3. *"MnO₂'s oxidising power is real: Mn(III) is about 25% of the manganese in the electrodeposited film, and Mn(III)/Mn(II) is 1.5 V, higher than Ir(VI)/Ir(IV) at 1.3 V. So whether MnO₂ buffers or over-oxidises our metal depends on the interface and the operating potential — which is why I will measure the potential window and the Ru valence first."*

## 四、我要向导师问的三个问题（会后或小会）

1. *"Could you also share the Nat. Catal. 2024 paper (ref. 33, Kong et al.) on the electrodeposited MnO₂ platform? I'd like to copy the deposition protocol."* —— 这是**直接可照做的电沉积步骤**。
2. *"In that work MnO₂ is consumed as the oxidant during synthesis. For our case, do you want Mn to act as an oxidant during synthesis, as an in-operando charge buffer, or both?"* —— 这一问能直接对齐课题定位（我的假设是**运行期缓冲**）。
3. *"Should I use the Mn(III) fraction as the control variable — i.e. tune deposition charge and pH to set the Mn(III)/Mn(IV) ratio, and track it by Mn K-edge XAS or Mn 3s XPS?"* —— 把"工艺可控性"讲成**可测量**的创新点。
