## 逐页质询

## P1 · 封面（*Stabilizing Ruthenium through Ru–O–Mn Interfacial Coupling*）

**🟡 Q1. "Why ruthenium rather than iridium?"**
> **A:** Iridium is the industry benchmark, but it is one of the scarcest elements and even IrO₂ dissolves as soluble [IrO₄]²⁻ at high potential. Ruthenium is far cheaper and more abundant, and it is *more* active — its weakness is stability, which is precisely the problem I want to solve. **(CSC-4 §4.4, p. 2208)**
> **答：** 铱是工业基准，但它是最稀缺的元素之一，而且高电位下连 IrO₂ 都会以可溶的 [IrO₄]²⁻ 溶解。钌便宜、丰富得多，而且**更活泼**——它的短板是稳定性，而这正是我要解决的问题。（CSC-4 §4.4, p.2208）

**🔴 Q2. "What does 'interfacial coupling' mean physically — what bond are you making?"**
> **A:** A shared oxygen bridge between ruthenium and manganese — Ru–O–Mn — that is, ruthenium anchored inside the MnO₂ lattice rather than sitting on top of it. The direct test is EXAFS: a Ru–O–Mn coordination shell appears while the Ru–O–Ru correlation of rutile-like RuO₂ weakens or disappears. **(cf. the Ir–O–Mn = 2.92 Å criterion in *Science* 2024, p. 666)**
> **答：** 是一条**共用的氧桥 Ru–O–Mn**——钌锚定在 MnO₂ 晶格**里面**，而不是躺在表面。直接判据是 EXAFS：**出现 Ru–O–Mn 配位壳、同时类金红石 RuO₂ 的 Ru–O–Ru 相关峰减弱或消失**。（对照 *Science* 2024 p.666 的 Ir–O–Mn = 2.92 Å 判据）

**🟢 Q3. "Basic science or applied? What does success look like?"**
> **A:** Both. The deliverable is an acid-stable anode at low noble-metal loading, and success has two parts: activity not worse than a RuO₂ baseline, and **lower dissolution** proven by ICP-MS and a stability number, plus one device-level durability number.
> **答：** 两者都有。产出是一个**低贵金属载量、酸性稳定**的阳极；成功的判据有两半：活性不低于 RuO₂ 基线，且**溶解更低**（用 ICP-MS 与稳定数证明），再加一个器件级的耐久数据。

## P3 · Background — *Acidic water electrolysis: the bottleneck is durability*

**🟡 Q1. "Why acidic at all? Alkaline lets you use cheap non-noble metals."**
> **A:** Because PEM is the technology that matches intermittent renewables — high current density, fast response, very pure hydrogen — and that is where my group's question lives: acidic OER at low noble-metal loading. Alkaline has its own constraints, and it is not the platform I am asked to work on.
> **答：** 因为 PEM 是与间歇性可再生能源匹配的技术——高电流密度、响应快、氢气纯度高——而组里的问题正是"**低贵金属载量下的酸性析氧**"。碱性有它自己的限制，也不是分配给我的平台。

**🔴 Q2. "You say only Ir and Ru oxides survive — but IrO₂ dissolves too. So what exactly is the constraint?"**
> **A:** The constraint is **durability at low noble-metal loading**, not activity. Both Ir and Ru oxides dissolve in acid; iridium is also one of the scarcest elements, so the only viable direction is to lower the loading while keeping the anode alive. That is the problem my project sits in. **(CSC-4 §4.4, p. 2208)**
> **答：** 约束是**低贵金属载量下的耐久性**，不是活性。铱和钌的氧化物在酸里都会溶解，而铱又极其稀缺，所以唯一可行的方向是**一边降载量、一边让阳极活着**。我的课题就在这个问题里。

**🟡 Q3. "What is your target metric — overpotential or lifetime?"**
> **A:** Overpotential is only a gate: I need η10 comparable to a RuO₂ baseline. The **discriminating** metric is dissolution — Ru and Mn dissolution rates measured separately by segmented ICP-MS, and a stability number — plus one device-level number: voltage at a given current density for a given time, with a decay rate.
> **答：** 过电位只是门槛：η10 要和 RuO₂ 基线可比。**真正区分优劣的是溶解**——用分段 ICP-MS 分别测钌和锰的溶解速率、给出稳定数——再加一个器件级数据：给定电流密度下的电压、运行时长与衰减率。

## P4 · Background — *The Ru paradox: more active and cheaper, but less stable*

**🔴 Q1. "Explain the two failure channels. Which one dominates in acid?"**
> **A:** Channel one is over-oxidation: ruthenium goes past +4 towards soluble RuO₄ and leaves the electrode. Channel two is lattice-oxygen participation — the lattice oxygen of the oxide is consumed by the reaction, so the metal follows the oxygen out. Which dominates depends on potential and on the surface structure; both are reported, which is why my design has to address both. **(AEM-5 §6, p. 15)**
> **答：** 通道一是**过氧化**：钌越过 +4 走向可溶的 RuO₄ 离开电极。通道二是**晶格氧参与（LOM）**：氧化物的晶格氧被反应消耗，金属跟着氧一起走。哪个主导取决于电位与表面结构，两者都有报道——所以我的设计必须同时针对这两条。（AEM-5 §6, p.15）

**🔴 Q2. "The O p-band trade-off: if moving the band up buys activity at the cost of stability, why not move it down and accept lower activity?"**
> **A:** Because then activity drops below the RuO₂ baseline and the material is useless for a PEM anode — the trade-off has to be **broken, not traded**. My hypothesis is to let manganese carry the redox flexibility and the proton-acceptor role, so ruthenium does not need to be driven to a high oxidation state to stay active. **(CSC-4 §2.2, p. 2202; R2 for the design claim)**
> **答：** 因为那样活性会掉到 RuO₂ 基线以下，做 PEM 阳极就没有意义了——这个权衡要**打破，而不是交换**。我的假设是**让锰承担变价与质子受体的角色**，这样钌不必被推到高氧化态也能保持活性。（CSC-4 §2.2, p.2202；设计主张属 R2）

**🟢 Q3. "Is RuO₄ formation thermodynamic or kinetic?"**
> **A:** Thermodynamics sets the ceiling — RuO₂ can be oxidised to RuO₄ at high potential — while kinetics controls how fast the intermediate high-valence states form and how the surface reconstructs. My design attacks both: keep Ru near +4 (thermodynamic ceiling) and control the local structure through the Ru–O–Mn bridge (kinetics).
> **答：** 热力学决定**天花板**（高电位下 RuO₂ 可以被氧化成 RuO₄），动力学决定**中间高价态生成多快、表面如何重构**。我的设计两边都打：把钌稳在 +4 附近（热力学天花板），并用 Ru–O–Mn 桥控制局域结构（动力学）。

## P5 · Literature I — *Design principles, and why MnO₂ is allowed*

**🔴 Q1. "Where does 0.37 V come from — exactly?"**
> **A:** From the scaling relation between the two intermediates: ΔG(*OOH) − ΔG(*OH) is fixed at about 3.2 eV. Out of the total 4.92 eV, that leaves 1.72 eV for two steps, 0.86 eV each — 0.37 V away from the 1.23 V equilibrium. In my schematic the largest step is 1.60 eV, and 1.60 − 1.23 = 0.37 V. **(CSC-4 §3.2, pp. 2203–2204)** The bar heights themselves are schematic; only 0.37 V is a literature number.
> **答：** 来自两个中间体之间的标度关系：**ΔG(\*OOH) − ΔG(\*OH) 被固定在约 3.2 eV**。总能量 4.92 eV 里这一步占掉 3.2 eV，剩下 1.72 eV 分给两步、各 0.86 eV——离 1.23 V 平衡值差 **0.37 V**。我图上最大台阶是 1.60 eV，1.60 − 1.23 = 0.37 V。（CSC-4 §3.2, pp.2203–2204）**柱高是示意，只有 0.37 V 是文献数字。**

**🔴 Q2. "The proton-acceptor route was demonstrated with Ni and Co in RuO₂. Why should manganese behave the same way?"**
> **A:** I do not claim it does — I claim it is the design hypothesis worth testing. The supporting argument is electronic: Mn³⁺ is reported to provide the optimal eg occupancy, eg = 1, for oxide OER, and manganese has an accessible Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ couple that can act as the proton acceptor and charge buffer. **(CSC-4 §2.2, p. 2200; R2 for the transfer to Mn)** If it fails, the go/no-go tells me within three weeks.
> **答：** 我不主张它一定相同——我主张这是**值得验证的设计假设**。支持理由是电子结构：文献报道 **Mn³⁺ 提供最优的 eg 占据数（eg = 1）**，且锰有可及的 Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ 电对可充当质子受体与电荷缓冲。（CSC-4 §2.2, p.2200；迁移到 Mn 属 R2）如果不成，go/no-go 三周内就会给出答案。

**🔴 Q3. "γ-MnO₂ running 8000 hours at pH 2 — at what potential, and at what loading?"**
> **A:** The review reports the lifetime but the honest answer is that it is a specific potential and loading, and the same review attaches the warning that **"a suitable potential window must be identified"**. That is why my first experiment is to measure the window for my own film rather than inherit someone else's. **(CSC-4 §4.4, p. 2208)**
> **答：** 综述报道的是寿命，但诚实的回答是：那是**特定电位与特定载量**下的结果，而且同一页明确写着**"必须先确定合适的电位窗口"**。所以我的第一个实验是**测我自己膜的窗口**，而不是继承别人的。（CSC-4 §4.4, p.2208）

**🟡 Q4. "Everyone knows MnO₂ is unstable in acid. Why do you think it is allowed?"**
> **A:** Because "unstable" is a statement about a window, not about the material: below about 1.4 V vs SHE manganese dissolves as Mn²⁺, above about 1.6 V permanganate can form, and in between the dioxide is stable. The question is whether that window is compatible with the OER potential and with the local environment — which is measurable, and that is slide 9.
> **答：** 因为"不稳定"说的是**窗口**而不是材料本身：低于约 1.4 V（vs SHE）锰以 Mn²⁺ 溶解，高于约 1.6 V 可能生成高锰酸根，中间二氧化锰是稳定的。问题是这个窗口与析氧电位、与局部环境是否兼容——这是**可以实测的**，也就是第 9 页。

## P6 · Literature II — *Reconstruction chemistry and Ru stabilisation in acid*

**🔴 Q1. "What is the real active phase, and what does that mean for your experiment?"**
> **A:** The reconstructed oxyhydroxide, MOOH — the film I deposit is only a pre-catalyst. Practically it means every characterisation is done as a **paired before/after activation** measurement, because the difference is what tells me what changed. **(AEM-5 §3, p. 6)**
> **答：** 是重构后的**羟基氧化物 MOOH**——我沉积的膜只是**预催化剂**。实践含义是：所有表征都做**活化前/活化后配对**，因为"差异"才能说明什么变了。（AEM-5 §3, p.6）

**🟡 Q2. "Then why deposit an oxide at all? Why not deposit the oxyhydroxide directly?"**
> **A:** Because the pre-catalyst determines what the reconstruction produces — its phase, its doping, its edge-site density. Electrodeposition gives me control over that template, which is the point of the project.
> **答：** 因为**预催化剂决定了重构产物**——它的物相、掺杂、边缘位密度。电沉积让我能控制这个"模板"，这正是课题的着力点。

**🟡 Q3. "CV or CP for activation? Does it matter?"**
> **A:** Yes — cyclic voltammetry drives complete oxidation while chronopotentiometry only gives partial oxidation, and full reconstruction can take eight hours or more. So the activation protocol is an experimental variable, not a step; I fix one protocol for all comparisons so that comparisons are fair. **(AEM-5, p. 6)**
> **答：** 有差别——**CV 驱动完全氧化，CP 只能部分氧化**，完全重构可能需要八小时以上。所以**活化程序是变量而不是步骤**；为了比较公平，我全课题固定一套程序。（AEM-5, p.6）

**🔴 Q4. "The Ru/α-MnO₂ result — 161 mV, more than 200 hours — is that your group's data?"**
> **A:** No. It is recorded *as reviewed in our group's review* — a precedent by other authors that the review summarises; I cite it as prior art, not as our result. **(AEM-5 §6, pp. 15–16)**
> **答：** 不是。它是**我们组那篇综述里记载的他人工作**——作为先例被综述收录；我引用它是作为**已有工作**，不是我们的结果。（AEM-5 §6, pp.15–16）

**🔴 Q5. "If that precedent already works, what is left for you to do?"**
> **A:** The precedent proved the chemistry — cation exchange, reconstructed Ru sites, the oxide path mechanism, no oxygen vacancies. What it does **not** provide is process control: nobody has shown how deposition charge, current and bath pH set the density and configuration of the coupling, and the dissolution metrics are missing across the whole field. That is my contribution.
> **答：** 先例证明了**化学可行**——阳离子交换、重构出 Ru 位点、走氧化物路径机制、不生成氧空位。它**没有**提供的是**工艺可控性**：没有人说明沉积电量、电流、浴 pH 如何决定耦合的密度与构型；而且整个领域都缺**溶解类指标**。这就是我的贡献。

## P7 · Literature III — *Interface environment, doping practice, device-level proof*

**🔴 Q1. "NE-2 is alkaline seawater. Why are you citing it for an acid system?"**
> **A:** I take the **methodology**, not the mechanism: it shows that the local environment around the active site is a design variable that can be quantified — with a rotating-ring-disk electrode for interfacial pH, in-situ infrared and DFT. And the lesson transfers in the opposite direction for us: in acid the anode generates protons locally, so the local pH is **lower** than the bulk, which makes stability harder, not easier. That is an argument for measuring rather than assuming.
> **答：** 我取的是**方法学**而不是机制：它证明活性位点**周围的局部环境是可以被量化设计的变量**——用旋转环盘电极定量界面 pH，配原位红外与 DFT。而且这个教训在我们这里**方向相反**：酸性下阳极会**局部产生质子**，局部 pH 比本体**更低**，稳定性更难而不是更容易——这正说明"必须实测，不能假设"。

**🔴 Q2. "N_D = 0.37 — what is that number?"**
> **A:** It is the **collection efficiency** of the ring-disk electrode, used to convert the ring current into an interfacial pH. It is **not** an electron-transfer number and **not** a selectivity. **(NE-2, Methods p. 270)**
> **答：** 那是旋转环盘电极的**收集效率**，用来把环电流换算成界面 pH。**不是**电子转移数，**也不是**选择性。（NE-2, Methods p.270）

**🟡 Q3. "You use 'AEM' twice, for two different things."**
> **A:** Correct, and I will say the full term each time: on slide 5, AEM = **adsorbate evolution mechanism**; in NC-1's context, AEM = **anion-exchange-membrane** electrolyser.
> **答：** 是的，我每次都念全称：第 5 页的 AEM = **吸附质演化机制**；NC-1 语境里的 AEM = **阴离子交换膜**电解槽。

**🟡 Q4. "Science-3 is a POM–MOF. How is it relevant?"**
> **A:** It supplies one design principle: a protecting unit does not have to be inert — a **variable-valence second metal can buffer the oxidative charge** and protect the active centre. That is precisely the role I assign to Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺.
> **答：** 它提供一条设计原则：保护单元**不必是惰性的**——**变价的第二金属可以缓冲氧化电荷**、保护活性中心。这正是我指派给 Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ 的角色。

**🔴 Q5. "So what is the gap, in one sentence?"**
> **A:** Principles, chemistry, interface environment and device practice are all established; **how to build the Ru–MnO₂ interface controllably and scalably is not.**
> **答：** 原理、化学、界面环境与器件实践都已建立；**"如何可控且可放大地构建 Ru–MnO₂ 界面"还没有。**

## P8 · Gap & hypothesis — *electrodeposition as the process lever*

**🔴 Q1. "Route A versus Route B — what is the difference in the material?"**
> **A:** Route A is a MnO₂ shell over a RuO₂ core: a coating, and a coating is only as good as its weakest component — the shell dissolves first and then the coating collapses. Route B puts ruthenium **inside** the MnO₂ framework so the two **share oxygen**; the stabilising element cannot leave independently of the structure.
> **答：** Route A 是 RuO₂ 核外包一层 MnO₂ 壳：**包覆层的强度取决于最弱的一环**——壳先溶，包覆层就崩。Route B 把钌**放进** MnO₂ 骨架里，让两者**共享氧**；稳定元素无法抛开结构单独离开。

**🔴 Q2. "Mechanistically, how does manganese keep ruthenium at +4? Give me the electron bookkeeping."**
> **A:** The Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ couple plus surface hydroxyls act as the electron reservoir: an oxidative equivalent is taken up by manganese — Mn³⁺ oxidised to Mn⁴⁺ — instead of being taken from ruthenium, so ruthenium stays near +4 and stays out of the RuO₄ window; the shared oxygen bridge keeps the local coordination rigid enough to hold the site. **(R2: this is my hypothesis, supported by the charge-buffering concept in Science-3 and the Mn redox chemistry in *Science* 2024)**
> **答：** **Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ 电对加上表面羟基充当电子库**：氧化当量由锰接住（Mn³⁺ → Mn⁴⁺），而不是从钌身上拿走，于是钌留在 +4 附近、不进 RuO₄ 窗口；共享氧桥让局域配位足够刚性、把位点钉住。（**R2：这是我的假设**，依据是 Science-3 的电荷缓冲概念与 *Science* 2024 的锰氧化还原化学）

**🔴🔴 Q3. "What deposition charge will you use, and why that value?"（本页最高危）**
> **A:** The MnO₂ host is electrodeposited **anodically at 7 mA cm⁻² for 15 minutes, i.e. about 6.3 C cm⁻²**, which by Faraday's law is roughly 2.8 mg cm⁻² of MnO₂, a few micrometres thick — that is the thickness needed to have interlayer sites for the metal. The 0.5–10 mC cm⁻² range printed on the slide refers to the **ruthenium loading**, in the near-single-atom regime; the two numbers are different quantities and I will label them separately.
> **答：** MnO₂ 宿主是**阳极沉积 7 mA cm⁻² × 15 min，即约 6.3 C cm⁻²**；按法拉第定律约 2.8 mg cm⁻² 的 MnO₂、微米级厚度——这才有"层间"位点容纳金属。PPT 上写的 0.5–10 mC cm⁻² 指的是**钌的载量**（近单原子量级）；**两个数不是同一件事，我会分别标注。**
> ⚠️ 排练提示：如果被追问，**不要为 0.5–10 mC cm⁻² 辩护成 MnO₂ 电量**；主动说清两者的区别，反而显示你算过账。

**🟡 Q4. "Which ruthenium route will you try first?"**
> **A:** Oxidative incorporation into the pre-formed host — a cation-exchange-type dip — with impregnation as the weak-coupling control and co-deposition as the fast alternative. The substituted-versus-impregnated comparison is what isolates the contribution of the interface.
> **答：** 先在已成型的宿主上做**氧化性嵌入（阳离子交换类）**；**浸渍**作为弱耦合对照，**共沉积**作为快速路线。**"取代 vs 浸渍"这一组对照**才是分离出界面贡献的关键。

**🟢 Q5. "How will you know ruthenium really entered the framework?"**
> **A:** Three checks: EXAFS — a Ru–O–Mn shell appears while the Ru–O–Ru correlation of rutile-like RuO₂ weakens; XPS/XANES — the ruthenium valence moves towards +4 rather than higher; and the material-level control — substituted samples must outperform impregnated ones.
> **答：** 三重检验：**EXAFS**——出现 Ru–O–Mn 壳层、同时类金红石的 Ru–O–Ru 相关减弱；**XPS/XANES**——钌价态趋向 +4 而不是更高；**材料级对照**——取代样必须优于浸渍样。

## P9 · Risk & go/no-go

**🔴 Q1. "Where do 1.4 and 1.6 V come from?"**
> **A:** From manganese Pourbaix thermodynamics: below about 1.4 V vs SHE manganese dissolves as Mn²⁺, roughly between 1.4 and 1.6 V the dioxide is stable, and above about 1.6 V permanganate can form. The figure is drawn as a schematic; the literature statement behind it is that **"a suitable potential window must be identified"** — the review's warning, not my measurement. **(CSC-4 §4.4, p. 2208)**
> **答：** 来自锰的 Pourbaix 热力学：低于约 1.4 V（vs SHE）锰以 Mn²⁺ 溶解，约 1.4–1.6 V 二氧化锰稳定，高于约 1.6 V 可能生成高锰酸根。图是**示意**；背后的文献原话是**"必须先确定合适的电位窗口"**——那是综述的警告，不是我的测量。（CSC-4 §4.4, p.2208）

**🟡 Q2. "Why start the potential step scan at 1.40 V?"**
> **A:** Because below that the dioxide is not stable anyway, so those points carry no information, and 1.90 V covers the high-current PEM regime. Fixed steps give me a **dissolution rate per potential** rather than a single "it dissolved" observation.
> **答：** 因为再往下二氧化锰本来就不稳定，测了没有信息量；1.90 V 覆盖 PEM 的高电流工况。**固定步长**能得到"**每个电位的溶解速率**"，而不是一个笼统的"它溶了没有"。

**🔴 Q3. "Your rule says 'much faster'. How much is much faster?"**
> **A:** I will use an explicit criterion so the decision is not a judgement call: within the same potential segment, a manganese dissolution rate at least **five times** the ruthenium rate with **no passivation trend** — or detection of the permanganate band — is a no-go. Otherwise we proceed.
> **答：** 我会用**可辩护的量化判据**，不让它变成主观判断：同一电位段内，锰的溶解速率**≥ 钌的 5 倍**且**不出现钝化趋势**——或者**检出高锰酸根谱带**——判为 no-go；否则继续。

**🔴 Q4. "Why measure ruthenium and manganese separately?"**
> **A:** Because a total dissolution number cannot tell me *which* failure I have: interface failure (Mn leaving) versus coating detachment. The two failures have completely different next steps, so I need the two elements resolved in the same experiment.
> **答：** 因为只测总量**分不清失效模式**：是界面失效（锰先走）还是涂层脱落。这两种失效的下一步完全不同，所以必须在**同一个实验里把两种元素分开测**。

**🟡 Q5. "How do you know the 839 cm⁻¹ band is permanganate?"**
> **A:** 839–840 cm⁻¹ is the symmetric Mn–O stretching mode of the permanganate ion, MnO₄⁻, and it has been used to follow manganese dissolution in operando Raman studies of manganese electrochemistry. It tells me the **pathway** — oxidative dissolution — not just the fact that something dissolved.
> **答：** 839–840 cm⁻¹ 是高锰酸根离子 MnO₄⁻ 的 **Mn–O 对称伸缩振动**，在锰电化学的 operando 拉曼研究里被用来追踪锰的溶解。它告诉我**路径**——是氧化溶解——而不只是"有东西溶了"。

**🟡 Q6. "Why those four controls?"**
> **A:** MnO₂ only gives the host baseline, RuO₂ gives the metal baseline, impregnated is weak coupling and substituted is strong coupling — so any improvement can be attributed to the interface rather than to either component alone.
> **答：** 只有 MnO₂ 给宿主基线，RuO₂ 给金属基线，浸渍是弱耦合、取代是强耦合——这样任何改进都可以归因到**界面**，而不是任一单一组分。

**🔴 Q7. "If the answer is no-go, then what?"**
> **A:** Three branches, in order: change the MnO₂ phase — α, β, γ or δ differ in tunnel structure and edge-site density; change the oxygen coordination by doping to raise the oxygen-vacancy formation energy; or introduce ruthenium first so that the MnO₂ grows around existing ruthenium sites. The point of the gate is that we choose a branch in week three instead of in month six.
> **答：** 三条分支，按顺序：**换相**（α/β/γ/δ 的隧道结构与边缘位密度不同）；**掺杂改氧配位**、提高氧空位形成能；或者**先引入钌**，让 MnO₂ 围绕已有钌位点生长。这个门的意义就是：**第三周做选择，而不是第六个月才发现**。

## P10 · Plan — *Synthesis, characterisation, testing and timeline*

**🔴 Q1. "Why electrodeposition rather than hydrothermal or impregnation?"**
> **A:** Because it gives me process control that the established syntheses do not: thickness is set by charge, it works near room temperature on a conductive substrate, it is scalable, and — most importantly for this project — the deposition parameters let me **scan** the density and configuration of the coupling. The literature provides the chemistry; it does not provide this control.
> **答：** 因为它提供既有合成法没有的**工艺可控性**：厚度由电量决定、接近室温、在导电基底上可放大；对本课题最关键的是——**沉积参数让我能"扫描"耦合的密度与构型**。文献给了化学，没给这种控制。

**🔴 Q2. "Operando XAS at the Ru and Mn K-edges — do you have beam time? At which facility?"**
> **A:** That is a gating item and I will not pretend otherwise: I will confirm the facility and the proposal window with my mentor before I enter the laboratory on 8 October. If beam time is not available early, the fallback is ex-situ paired XAS, EPR and XPS plus in-situ Raman, and the interface claim becomes a later milestone rather than a first-month one.
> **答：** 这是**卡口问题，我不假装它已解决**：我会在 10 月 8 日进实验室前与 mentor 确认机时来源与申请窗口。若前期拿不到机时，退路是**非原位配对 XAS + EPR + XPS + 原位拉曼**，把界面证据从"第一个月"挪到后续里程碑。

**🟡 Q3. "Why normalise by both mass and ECSA?"**
> **A:** Mass normalisation addresses the actual goal, which is activity per gram of noble metal; ECSA normalisation separates intrinsic activity from surface-area effects. If the two disagree, the reason is usually surface area, and I want to know that before claiming an improvement.
> **答：** 质量归一化对应真正的目标——**每克贵金属的活性**；ECSA 归一化把本征活性与比表面效应分开。两者若不一致，原因通常是比表面，我需要在宣称改进之前知道这一点。

**🟡 Q4. "What is a stability number?"**
> **A:** The number of oxygen molecules evolved per dissolved metal atom — a durability metric that turns "it lasted some hours" into a number that can be compared across catalysts. The assigned reviews report decay rates, not dissolution, so this is data I will generate.
> **答：** **每溶解一个金属原子所析出的氧分子数**——把"跑了多少小时"变成可横向比较的数字。五篇指定文献只报衰减率、不报溶解，所以这部分数据由我产生。

**🟡 Q5. "Why is a single-cell test necessary if you already have half-cell data?"**
> **A:** Because half-cell metrics can mislead: local pH, temperature, mass transport and membrane effects are all different in a device. All three of my device-level references ended with a device test, and I report it the same way they do — current density, duration, retention and Faradaic efficiency.
> **答：** 因为半电池指标会误导：器件里的**局部 pH、温度、传质与膜效应**都不一样。我引用的三篇器件级工作最后都做了单电池测试，我按同样方式报数——电流密度、时长、保持率与法拉第效率。
