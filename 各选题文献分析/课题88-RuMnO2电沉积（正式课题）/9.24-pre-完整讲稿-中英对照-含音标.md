## P3 · 背景一：酸性水电解的瓶颈是耐久性

**英文讲稿**

> PEM electrolysis matches intermittent renewables: high current density, fast response, high-purity hydrogen. The anode operates at about one molar proton activity, that is pH zero. Only iridium and ruthenium oxides are stable in this environment. Iridium is one of the scarcest elements, and even IrO2 dissolves as soluble [IrO4]2- at high potential. The main constraint is therefore durability at low noble-metal loading, not activity. The figure shows the cell; the anode is the acidic oxygen-evolving side, which is the interface studied here.

**中文对照**

> PEM 电解与间歇性可再生能源匹配：高电流密度、响应快、氢气纯度高。阳极在约 1 M 质子活度（即 pH 0）下运行。该环境中只有铱和钌的氧化物稳定。铱是最稀缺的元素之一，且高电位下连 IrO₂ 也会以可溶的 [IrO₄]²⁻ 溶解。因此主要约束是低贵金属载量下的耐久性，而不是活性。图中是电解槽示意；阳极即酸性析氧侧，也就是本课题研究的界面。

**台上提示**：指示意图；`durability at low noble-metal loading` 放慢。

## P4 · 背景二：Ru 的悖论——更活泼、更便宜，但更不稳定

**英文讲稿**

> Ruthenium was selected for two reasons: RuO2 is more active than IrO2 for oxygen evolution, and ruthenium is cheaper and more abundant than iridium. Its limitation is stability. Ruthenium fails through two channels. The first is over-oxidation: ruthenium is oxidised beyond +4 to soluble RuO4 and leaves the electrode. The second is lattice-oxygen participation: lattice oxygen is consumed by the reaction, and the metal is released with it. These channels are linked to a design trade-off: a higher O 2p band centre increases activity but decreases stability. The objective is therefore stabilisation, and a stabilisation result requires a dissolution measurement, not only an overpotential.

**中文对照**

> 选钌有两个原因：析氧反应中 RuO₂ 比 IrO₂ 更活泼；钌比铱便宜、储量更丰富。它的短板是稳定性。钌的失效有两条通道。第一条是过氧化：钌被氧化越过 +4 价，生成可溶 RuO₄ 并离开电极。第二条是晶格氧参与：晶格氧被反应消耗，金属随之脱离。两条通道与一个设计权衡相关：O 2p 带中心越高，活性越高、稳定性越低。因此目标是稳定化，而稳定化的结论需要溶解测量支持，不能只用过电位。

**台上提示**：指左框（两条通道）→ 指下方权衡框。两句结论放慢。

## P5 · 文献一：设计原则，以及为什么 MnO2 是被允许的

**英文讲稿**

> The review gives these design rules. The left figure shows the four steps of oxygen evolution. If every step had the same free energy, 1.23 electronvolts, the overpotential would be zero. Real surfaces differ: *OH and *OOH are connected by a scaling relation and differ in free energy by about 3.2 electronvolts, so the steps become uneven. The largest step here is 1.60 electronvolts, which is 0.37 volts above 1.23 volts. The right figure shows the volcano relation: activity peaks at an intermediate adsorption energy. The proton-acceptor route changes the mechanism: nickel or cobalt in RuO2 activates a bridging oxygen and lowers the determining step to 1.49 or 1.33 electronvolts, that is 0.26 or 0.10 volts. Two results support manganese: gamma-MnO2 has been reported to operate for 8,000 hours at pH 2, and Mn3+ provides the optimal eg occupancy, eg equal to one. The review adds one condition: a suitable potential window must be identified.

**中文对照**

> 综述给出的设计规则如下。左图是析氧的四个步骤。如果每一步自由能都等于 1.23 eV，过电位为零。真实表面并非如此：\*OH 与 \*OOH 之间存在标度关系，自由能相差约 3.2 eV，因此台阶不均匀。此处最大台阶为 1.60 eV，比 1.23 V 高 0.37 V。右图是火山关系：活性在中等吸附能处最高。质子受体路线改变机理：RuO₂ 中的镍或钴活化桥氧，把决速步降到 1.49 或 1.33 eV，即 0.26 或 0.10 V。两条结果支持用锰：γ-MnO₂ 被报道可在 pH 2 下运行 8,000 小时；Mn³⁺ 提供最优的 eg 占据数，eg 等于 1。综述还给出一个前提：必须先确定合适的电位窗口。

**台上提示**：点最大台阶 → 点火山顶 → 点 1.49/1.33 eV 注释；最后一句过渡到 P9。

## P6 · 文献二：重构化学，以及酸性中的 Ru 稳定化

**英文讲稿**

> The second paper concerns reconstruction. The deposited film is not the catalyst: during activation the surface converts to an oxyhydroxide, MOOH, which is the active phase. The review describes four reconstruction mechanisms and six factors controlling the activity of the reconstructed surface, including edge facets, vacancies, adsorbed anions and strain. Two points are relevant here. First, the activation protocol is a variable: cyclic voltammetry gives complete oxidation, chronopotentiometry gives partial oxidation, and full reconstruction can take eight hours. Second, in acid, ruthenium fails through RuO4 formation and lattice-oxygen participation, and two stabilisation routes are established: single-atom confinement, and an increase in the oxygen-vacancy formation energy. The review also records a precedent: ruthenium on alpha-MnO2 prepared by cation exchange, with 161 millivolts of overpotential and a lifetime above 200 hours.

**中文对照**

> 第二篇文献讨论重构。沉积得到的膜不是催化剂：活化过程中表面转变为羟基氧化物 MOOH，这才是活性相。综述描述了四种重构机制，以及控制重构表面活性的六个因素，包括边缘晶面、空位、吸附阴离子与应变。有两点与本课题相关。第一，活化程序是变量：循环伏安给出完全氧化，恒电流给出部分氧化，完全重构可能需八小时。第二，在酸性中钌通过生成 RuO₄ 与晶格氧参与失效，已有两条稳定化路线：单原子限域，以及提高氧空位形成能。综述还记载了一个先例：通过阳离子交换制备的 α-MnO₂ 负载钌，过电位 161 mV，寿命超过 200 小时。

**台上提示**：第一句重读；提先例必须说 `as recorded in our group's review`。

## P7 · 文献三：界面环境、掺杂实践、器件级验证

**英文讲稿**

> Three results from the remaining papers. First, the local environment around the active site controls stability: a hard-Lewis-acid layer keeps the local pH near 14 at 1.6 volts, giving more than 200 hours in seawater with about 98 per cent oxygen selectivity. The local environment is therefore a design variable, distinct from the bulk. Second, doping stabilises the metal, and the work is not complete until it is tested in a device: a chromium-doped amorphous oxide gives 268 millivolts, and the electrolyser runs for 120 hours with a decay below 4.9 millivolts per hour. Third, a protecting unit can be redox-active: a variable-valence second metal buffers the oxidative charge. The gap is therefore not the chemistry but the construction of the interface: how to build Ru-O-Mn controllably and at a larger scale.

**中文对照**

> 其余三篇各一条结果。第一，活性位点周围的局部环境决定稳定性：硬路易斯酸层在 1.6 V 下把局部 pH 保持在 14 附近，从而在海水中获得超过 200 小时寿命和约 98% 的氧气选择性。因此局部环境是一个设计变量，与本体不同。第二，掺杂可稳定金属，且工作必须做到器件验证才算完成：铬掺杂非晶氧化物过电位 268 mV，电解槽运行 120 小时，衰减低于 4.9 mV h⁻¹。第三，保护单元可以是变价的：变价第二金属缓冲氧化电荷。因此缺口不在化学，而在界面的构建——如何可控且更大规模地构建 Ru–O–Mn。

**台上提示**：三条各一句结论 + 一个数字。器件那句说全称 `anion-exchange-membrane electrolyser`。

## P8 · 缺口与假设：电沉积作为工艺杠杆

**英文讲稿**

> Route A is a MnO2 shell around a RuO2 core; the shell dissolves first, and the coating then fails. Route B places ruthenium inside the MnO2 framework, so the two share oxygen and form Ru-O-Mn. The material hypothesis is that electrodeposited MnO2 acts as support and redox mediator: the Mn3+/Mn4+ couple and surface hydroxyl groups absorb oxidative charge and hold ruthenium near +4, outside the RuO4 window. The process hypothesis is that deposition parameters control the phase, morphology and edge-site density, so the coupling density and configuration can be scanned. The variables are the host deposition at 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes, about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre, with a two-fold gradient; the current and the bath pH; and the ruthenium route: co-deposition, impregnation or cation exchange. This process control is not available in the literature.

**中文对照**

> Route A 是在 RuO₂ 核外包一层 MnO₂ 壳：壳先溶解，包覆层随后失效。Route B 把钌放进 MnO₂ 骨架内部，使两者共享氧，形成 Ru–O–Mn。材料假设：电沉积 MnO₂ 同时作为载体与氧化还原介质——Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ 电对与表面羟基吸收氧化电荷，使钌保持在 +4 附近、处于 RuO₄ 窗口之外。工艺假设：沉积参数控制物相、形貌与边缘位密度，因此耦合的密度与构型可被扫描。变量包括：宿主沉积为 7 mA cm⁻² 持续 15 分钟，约 6.3 C cm⁻²，设两倍梯度；电流与浴 pH；以及钌的引入路线——共沉积、浸渍或阳离子交换。这种工艺可控性在现有文献中没有。

**台上提示**：Route A / Route B 分开指；`support and redox mediator`、`not available in the literature` 放慢。

## P9 · 主要风险，以及我们的 go / no-go 实验

**英文讲稿**

> The largest risk is manganese, not ruthenium. In acid, MnO2 is stable only in a narrow potential range: below about 1.4 volts manganese dissolves as Mn2+; between 1.4 and 1.6 volts the dioxide is stable; above about 1.6 volts soluble permanganate can form. Oxygen evolution requires more than 1.23 volts, so the overlap is small. The review states that this window must be identified. If the window shifts under load, the coating dissolves before the ruthenium beneath it.
>
> The first experiment is a decision test in weeks one to three, with four parts: a potential-step dissolution scan from 1.40 to 1.90 volts against RHE, with each step collected separately; segmented ICP-MS, with ruthenium and manganese measured separately; in-situ Raman, for the permanganate band near 839 reciprocal centimetres; and four controls: MnO2 only, RuO2, impregnated and substituted. The decision rule is on the slide: if manganese dissolves much faster than ruthenium and cannot be suppressed, the MnO2 phase or the oxygen coordination will be changed, or ruthenium introduced first; otherwise the work continues to interface engineering and the parameter sweep.

**中文对照**

> 最大的风险是锰，不是钌。在酸性中 MnO₂ 只在很窄的电位区间内稳定：低于约 1.4 V，锰以 Mn²⁺ 溶解；1.4 至 1.6 V 之间二氧化锰稳定；高于约 1.6 V 可生成可溶的高锰酸根。析氧需要高于 1.23 V，因此重叠区间很小。综述指出必须确定这一窗口。若窗口在负载下移动，包覆层会在其下方的钌之前溶解。
>
> 第一个实验是前三周的判定实验，含四部分：电位阶跃溶出扫描，1.40 至 1.90 V（vs RHE），每段单独收集；分段 ICP-MS，钌与锰分开测量；原位拉曼，检测 839 cm⁻¹ 附近的高锰酸根谱带；以及四个对照：只有 MnO₂、RuO₂、浸渍、取代。判定规则在幻灯片上：若锰的溶解明显快于钌且无法抑制，则改变 MnO₂ 物相或氧配位，或先引入钌；否则继续进入界面工程与参数扫描。

**台上提示**：`manganese, not ruthenium` 重读；左图三段电位沿横轴指。结尾补量化口径：同电位段内 Mn 速率 ≥ Ru 的 5 倍且无钝化，或出现 839 cm⁻¹ 谱带 → no-go。

## P10 · 合成、表征、测试与时间线

**英文讲稿**

> The plan has four parts. Synthesis: MnO2 is electrodeposited on titanium mesh or felt at 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes, about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre; current and bath pH are variables; ruthenium is introduced at a near-single-atom level, with one fixed activation protocol. Characterisation: paired XRD, Raman, XPS and HRTEM-EDS before and after activation; operando XAS at the ruthenium and manganese K-edges, decisive for Ru-O-Mn; and EPR for oxygen vacancies. Testing: iR-corrected LSV for the overpotential at 10 milliamps per square centimetre, Tafel slope and EIS, normalised by mass and by ECSA; and long-term chronopotentiometry with segmented ICP-MS, reported with a stability number. Device: one single-cell test, reported as current density, duration and retention, with Faradaic efficiency. Milestones: laboratory entry on 8 October 2026, mid-term review on 6 January 2027, final report in April 2027.

**中文对照**

> 方案分四部分。合成：MnO₂ 电沉积在钛网或钛毡上，7 mA cm⁻² 持续 15 分钟，约 6.3 C cm⁻²；电流与浴 pH 为变量；钌以近单原子载量引入；固定一套活化程序。表征：活化前后配对 XRD、拉曼、XPS 与 HRTEM-EDS；在钌与锰 K 边做 operando XAS，这是判断 Ru–O–Mn 的决定性测量；用 EPR 测氧空位。测试：iR 校正 LSV 给出 10 mA cm⁻² 下的过电位、Tafel 斜率与 EIS，用质量与 ECSA 归一化；长时恒电流配分段 ICP-MS，并给出稳定数。器件：一次单电池测试，按电流密度、时长与保持率报数，并给出法拉第效率。里程碑：2026 年 10 月 8 日进实验室，2027 年 1 月 6 日中期评审，2027 年 4 月最终报告。

### 13.5 最容易被听错／念错的六个

## §14 两个必须在口播时说全称的歧义

1. **AEM**
   - P5 左图与 CSC-4 语境 = **adsorbate evolution mechanism**（吸附质演化机制），与 LOM 相对；
   - P7／P11 与 NC-1 语境 = **anion exchange membrane**（阴离子交换膜）电解槽。
   - 口播时说全称："the adsorbate evolution mechanism" vs "an anion-exchange-membrane electrolyser"。同一场报告里混用会让人以为在说同一件事。
2. **N_D = 0.37**（附表 NE-2 条目）
   - 它是 **RRDE 的收集效率（collection efficiency）**，用于把环电流换算成界面 pH；
   - **不是电子转移数，也不是选择性**。被问到就照这句答。
