## P3 · 背景一：酸性水电解的瓶颈是耐久性

**英文讲稿**

> Let me start with why this project exists.
>
> PEM water electrolysis is the technology that couples well with intermittent renewables: it runs at high current density, it responds fast, and it delivers very pure hydrogen. The catch is the environment at the anode: roughly **one molar proton activity** — in other words, pH zero. In that environment, almost no transition-metal oxide survives. In practice, only iridium and ruthenium oxides do.
>
> And iridium is among the scarcest elements on earth. Even iridium oxide is not inert — at high potential it dissolves as the soluble complex **[IrO4]2-**. So the real constraint on the cost of a PEM stack is **not activity; it is durability at low noble-metal loading**. That is the problem my project sits inside.
>
> [指向右侧示意图] On the right is a schematic of the cell. The anode is the acidic, oxygen-evolving side — and that anode interface is exactly what I will be working on.

**中文对照**

> 先说这个课题为什么存在。
>
> PEM 水电解是与间歇性可再生能源匹配得最好的技术：高电流密度、响应快、产氢纯度很高。问题出在阳极环境：大约**1 M 的质子活度**——也就是 pH 0。在这种环境里，几乎没有任何过渡金属氧化物能存活，实际上只有铱和钌的氧化物可以。
>
> 而铱是地球上最稀缺的元素之一。而且即便是氧化铱也不是惰性的——在高电位下它会以可溶配合物 **[IrO4]2-** 的形式溶解。所以 PEM 电解槽成本的真正约束**不是活性，而是低贵金属载量下的耐久性**。我的课题就落在这个问题里。
>
> [指向右侧示意图] 右边是电解槽示意图。阳极就是酸性、析氧的那一侧——而那个阳极界面，正是我要做工作的地方。

**台上提示**：这一段是**立论**，语速放慢。"not activity; it is durability" 中间停顿。指向示意图时手不要挡住图。

## P4 · 背景二：Ru 的悖论——更活泼、更便宜，但更不稳定

**英文讲稿**

> So why ruthenium?
>
> RuO2 is **more active** than IrO2 for the oxygen evolution reaction, and ruthenium is orders of magnitude **cheaper and more abundant** than iridium. The problem is that it is also the **least stable** of the two — and the failure has two distinct channels.
>
> [指向左框] Channel one: **over-oxidation**. Ruthenium goes beyond +4 to soluble **RuO4**, and it simply leaves the electrode. Channel two: **lattice-oxygen participation** — the lattice oxygen of the oxide is consumed in the reaction, so the metal follows the oxygen out.
>
> And these two channels are tied to a design trade-off: moving the **O p-band centre closer to the Fermi level** increases activity and decreases stability. Activity and stability are coupled through the same electronic descriptor.
>
> So the question for me is not "how do I make ruthenium more active". It is **"how do I keep ruthenium where it is"** — and any claim of stabilisation has to be demonstrated with a **dissolution-based metric**, not only with an overpotential.

**中文对照**

> 那为什么是钌？
>
> 对于析氧反应，RuO2 比 IrO2 **更活泼**，而钌比铱**便宜、丰富好几个数量级**。问题是它也是两者中**最不稳定**的——而且失效有两条不同的通道。
>
> [指向左框] 通道一：**过氧化**。钌从 +4 价被进一步氧化成可溶的 **RuO4**，直接离开电极。通道二：**晶格氧参与**——氧化物自身的晶格氧在反应中被消耗，于是金属跟着氧一起走。
>
> 这两条通道又和一个设计权衡绑定：**O 2p 带中心越靠近费米能级**，活性越高、稳定性越低。活性和稳定性被同一个电子结构描述符耦合在一起。
>
> 所以我的问题不是"怎么让钌更活泼"，而是**"怎么把钌留在原地"**——而且任何关于稳定化的主张，都必须用**溶解类指标**来证明，不能只用过电位。

**台上提示**：这一页是全场的"钩子"。"keep ruthenium where it is" 说完停一秒，这是你的课题主张。

## P5 · 文献一：设计原则，以及为什么 MnO2 是被允许的

**英文讲稿**

> This is my first literature message, and it has two halves.
>
> [指左图] On the left: the four steps of oxygen evolution on an oxide surface. If all four steps had the same free energy — 1.23 electronvolts each, the thermodynamic value — there would be no overpotential at all. Real oxide surfaces cannot do that: the intermediates \*OH and \*OOH **scale with each other**, so the profile is uneven. Here the largest step is 1.60 eV; the difference from 1.23 V is **0.37 V**, and that is the overpotential you cannot remove. It is the well-known **scaling limit**.
>
> [指右图] On the right: the volcano. Activity is highest when the adsorption energy is neither too weak nor too strong. The scaling relation fixes the **height** of that volcano. The way to break it is to change the **mechanism** — the so-called **proton-acceptor route**, where nickel or cobalt inside RuO2 activates a bridging oxygen, and the determining step drops to **1.49 or 1.33 eV — that is 0.26 or 0.10 V, below the 0.37 V limit**.
>
> And now the part that makes MnO2 legitimate for me: gamma-MnO2 has been **reported to run 8,000 hours at pH 2**, and **Mn3+ provides the optimal eg occupancy — eg equals one**. Both are design evidence, not a promise.
>
> But there is a warning attached to that same page: **"a suitable potential window must be identified"**. That warning is precisely what slide 9 is about.

**中文对照**

> 这是我的第一条文献信息，分两半。
>
> [指左图] 左边：氧化物表面析氧的四个步骤。如果四步的自由能都等于热力学值 1.23 eV，那就完全没有过电位。但真实的氧化物表面做不到：中间体 \*OH 与 \*OOH **彼此线性标度**，所以台阶是不均匀的。这里最大的一级台阶是 1.60 eV，与 1.23 V 的差是 **0.37 V**——这是你无法消除的过电位，也就是著名的**标度极限**。
>
> [指右图] 右边：火山图。当吸附能既不太弱也不太强时活性最高。标度关系决定的是这座火山的**高度**。打破它的办法是换**机理**——即所谓的**质子受体路线**：RuO2 中的镍或钴活化桥氧，决速步降到 **1.49 或 1.33 eV——也就是 0.26 或 0.10 V，低于 0.37 V 这个上限**。
>
> 接下来是让 MnO2 对我而言"合法"的部分：γ-MnO2 曾被报道**在 pH 2 下运行 8,000 小时**，而 **Mn3+ 提供最优的 eg 占据数——eg 等于 1**。这两条都是设计依据，不是承诺。
>
> 但同一页也带着一句警告：**"必须先确定合适的电位窗口"**。这句警告，正是第 9 页要处理的事情。

**台上提示**：这一页最长，1 分 40 秒。手要在两幅图之间明确移动；说 "0.37 V" 时手指点在最难那级台阶上。最后一句是**埋钩子**，直接连到 P9。

## P6 · 文献二：重构化学，以及酸性中的 Ru 稳定化

**英文讲稿**

> The second paper — the review — changed the way I think about the electrode.
>
> The film you deposit is **not the catalyst**. During activation, the surface **reconstructs**: it converts to an oxyhydroxide, MOOH, and *that* is the real active phase. [指图] Four reconstruction mechanisms are described, and **six factors** govern the activity of the reconstructed surface: exposed edge facets, residual dopant cations, unsaturated coordination and vacancies, adsorbed oxyanions, hybrid interfaces where bridging oxygen transfers electrons, and **strain**, which broadens the eg band.
>
> Two practical points I took from it. First, **the activation protocol is not a step — it is a variable**: cyclic voltammetry drives complete oxidation, chronopotentiometry only partial, and full reconstruction can take eight hours or more. Second, in acid, Ru fails through RuO4 and lattice oxygen, and **two stabilisation routes are already proven**: confining the metal as single-atom sites, and raising the oxygen-vacancy formation energy.
>
> And there is a **precedent recorded in that review**: Ru on alpha-MnO2 by cation exchange — 161 millivolts of overpotential, more than 200 hours. That precedent is the reason my hypothesis looks the way it does.

**中文对照**

> 第二篇——那篇综述——改变了我对电极的看法。
>
> 你沉积出来的膜**不是催化剂**。在活化过程中表面会**重构**：转变成羟基氧化物 MOOH，那才是真正的活性相。[指图] 文中描述了四种重构机制，以及支配重构表面活性的**六个因素**：暴露的边缘晶面、残留的掺杂阳离子、不饱和配位与空位、吸附的含氧阴离子、桥氧传递电子的杂化界面，以及使 eg 带展宽的**应变**。
>
> 我从中取了两条实践要点。第一，**活化程序不是一个步骤，而是一个变量**：循环伏安驱动完全氧化，恒电流只能部分氧化，而完全重构可能需要八小时以上。第二，在酸性中 Ru 通过 RuO4 和晶格氧失效，而**已有两条被验证的稳定化路线**：把金属限域成单原子位点，以及提高氧空位形成能。
>
> 而且那篇综述里**记载了一个先例**：通过阳离子交换把 Ru 放到 α-MnO2 上——过电位 161 mV，稳定性超过 200 小时。正是这个先例，决定了我的假设长成现在这个样子。

**台上提示**："the film you deposit is not the catalyst" 是这一页的题眼，说完停半秒。提到先例时必须说 **"as reviewed in our group's review"**（这是综述里记载的他人工作，不是我们组的数据）。

## P7 · 文献三：界面环境、掺杂实践、器件级验证

**英文讲稿**

> Three more papers — one lesson each.
>
> **NE-2**: what happens *around* the active site decides stability. A hard-Lewis-acid layer keeps the **local pH near 14 even at 1.6 V**; that gives more than 200 hours in natural seawater with about 98 percent oxygen selectivity. The lesson is that the local environment is a **design variable** — and it is not the same as the bulk.
>
> **NC-1**: doping stabilises the metal, and the paper is not finished until it is tested **in a device**. Chromium-doped amorphous oxide, 268 millivolts at 10 milliamps per square centimetre; then an anion-exchange-membrane electrolyser: 120 hours at 0.5 amps per square centimetre, with less than **4.9 mV per hour** of decay.
>
> **Science-3**: a protecting unit does **not have to be inert**. In that POM–MOF superstructure, a **variable-valence second metal buffers the oxidative charge** and protects the active centre.
>
> So: principles, chemistry, interface environment and device practice are all established. What is **not** established is how to **build** the Ru–MnO2 interface controllably and scalably. That is my gap.

**中文对照**

> 还有三篇——每篇一条教训。
>
> **NE-2**：活性位点**周围**发生的事情决定稳定性。硬路易斯酸层让**局部 pH 即使在 1.6 V 时也保持在 14 附近**；这带来了天然海水中超过 200 小时的稳定性，以及约 98% 的氧气选择性。教训是：局部环境是一个**设计变量**，而且它不等于本体环境。
>
> **NC-1**：掺杂能稳定金属，而且一篇工作必须做到**器件验证**才算完成。铬掺杂非晶氧化物，10 mA cm-2 时过电位 268 mV；然后是阴离子交换膜电解槽：0.5 A cm-2 下运行 120 小时，衰减小于 **4.9 mV h-1**。
>
> **Science-3**：保护单元**不必是惰性的**。在那个 POM–MOF 超结构里，**变价的第二金属缓冲氧化电荷**，从而保护活性中心。
>
> 所以：原理、化学、界面环境和器件实践都已经建立起来了。**没有**建立的是——如何**可控且可放大**地构建 Ru–MnO2 界面。这就是我的缺口。

**台上提示**：这一页三块，每块一句话结论 → 一句数据 → 停。注意 **AEM 说全称 anion-exchange-membrane**（不要与 P5 的 adsorbate evolution mechanism 混）。

## P8 · 缺口与假设：电沉积作为工艺杠杆

**英文讲稿**

> Here is the gap in one picture.
>
> [指 Route A] Route A — a MnO2 shell around a RuO2 core. It looks attractive, but think about the stability windows: the shell dissolves first, and then the coating collapses. **A coating is only as good as its weakest component.**
>
> [指 Route B] Route B — put ruthenium **into** the MnO2 framework, so the two share oxygen: **Ru–O–Mn coupling**. Then the Mn3+/Mn4+ couple and the surface hydroxyls can **buffer the oxidative charge** at Ru and hold Ru near +4 — which is **out of the RuO4 window**.
>
> So my material hypothesis is: electrodeposited MnO2 acts as **both support and redox mediator**. My **process** hypothesis is the part I actually control: deposition charge, current or potential, and bath pH set the phase, the morphology and the density of edge sites — so the **density and configuration of Ru–O–Mn coupling becomes a variable I can scan**.
>
> And that is exactly what the existing literature does not provide: the chemistry is there, the **process control** is not.

**中文对照**

> 缺口用一张图说清楚。
>
> [指 Route A] Route A——在 RuO2 核外面包一层 MnO2 壳。看起来很吸引人，但想想稳定窗口：壳会先溶解，然后整个包覆层就崩掉。**一个包覆层的强度取决于它最弱的那一环。**
>
> [指 Route B] Route B——把钌**放进** MnO2 骨架里，让两者共享氧：**Ru–O–Mn 耦合**。这样 Mn3+/Mn4+ 电对和表面羟基就能**缓冲 Ru 上的氧化电荷**，把 Ru 保持在 +4 附近——也就是**待在 RuO4 窗口之外**。
>
> 所以我的材料假设是：电沉积的 MnO2 **既是载体、也是氧化还原介质**。我的**工艺**假设则是我真正能控制的部分：沉积电荷量、电流或电位、浴 pH 决定物相、形貌和边缘位密度——于是 **Ru–O–Mn 耦合的密度与构型就变成了一个可以扫描的变量**。
>
> 而这恰恰是现有文献没有提供的：化学原理有了，**工艺可控性**没有。

**台上提示**：Route A / Route B 要用手明确分开指；"both support and redox mediator" 是你材料假设的一句话版本。"process control is not" 结束停一秒。

## P9 · 主要风险，以及我们的 go / no-go 实验

**英文讲稿**

> Now the honest part.
>
> The biggest risk in this project is **not ruthenium — it is the manganese**. [指左图] In acid, MnO2 is only thermodynamically stable in a **narrow window**: below about 1.4 volts versus SHE you get dissolved Mn2+; between roughly 1.4 and 1.6 volts the dioxide is stable; above about 1.6 volts you can go to **permanganate**, which is also soluble. Oxygen evolution requires more than 1.23 volts, so the **usable overlap is small** — and that is exactly what the review means by "a suitable potential window must be identified".
>
> If that window is too narrow, or shifts under load, then **a MnO2 coating would dissolve before the ruthenium underneath does**. That is the failure mode I have to rule out first.
>
> [指右卡] So the first experiment, in weeks one to three, is a **go / no-go**. We run a **potential-step dissolution scan** from 1.40 to 1.90 volts versus RHE, in fixed steps, so that every potential has its own dissolution rate; the electrolyte is collected **in segments** and measured by **ICP-MS**, and crucially we measure **ruthenium and manganese separately, in the same experiment**. In parallel, **in-situ Raman** looks for the permanganate band near **839 cm-1** — if it appears, we know at which potential it forms. And there are **four controls**: MnO2 only, RuO2, impregnated, and substituted.
>
> The decision rule is on the slide: **if manganese dissolves much faster than ruthenium and cannot be suppressed, we change the MnO2 phase or the oxygen coordination, or we dope ruthenium first. Otherwise, we proceed to interface engineering and the parameter sweep.**

**中文对照**

> 现在说诚实的部分。
>
> 这个课题最大的风险**不是钌，而是锰**。[指左图] 在酸性中，MnO2 只在**一个很窄的窗口**里热力学稳定：低于约 1.4 V（vs SHE）你得到溶解的 Mn2+；大约 1.4–1.6 V 之间二氧化锰稳定；高于约 1.6 V 会走向**高锰酸根**，那也是可溶的。析氧要求电位高于 1.23 V，所以**可用的重叠区间很小**——这正是综述说"必须先确定合适的电位窗口"的意思。
>
> 如果这个窗口太窄，或者在负载下发生移动，那么 **MnO2 包覆层会在下面的钌溶解之前就先溶解**。这就是我必须最先排除的失效模式。
>
> [指右卡] 所以前三周的第一个实验是一个 **go / no-go**。我们做**电位阶跃溶出扫描**，从 1.40 到 1.90 V（vs RHE），按固定步长，让每个电位都有自己的溶解速率；电解液**分段**收集，用 **ICP-MS** 测量，关键是在**同一个实验里把钌和锰分开测**。同时，**原位拉曼**去找 **839 cm-1** 附近的高锰酸根谱带——如果它出现，我们就知道是在哪个电位形成的。还有**四个对照**：只有 MnO2、RuO2、浸渍法、取代法。
>
> 判据就在幻灯片上：**如果锰的溶解明显快于钌且无法抑制，我们就换 MnO2 的物相或氧配位，或者先掺钌；否则，进入界面工程与参数扫描。**

**台上提示**：这是全场最重要的一页。"not ruthenium — it is the manganese" 要重读。左图三段电位讲解时手顺着横轴走。**主动给出 "much faster" 的量化口径**（同一电位段内 Mn 溶解速率 ≥ Ru 的 5 倍且无钝化趋势，或出现 839 cm-1 带 → no-go），这句话能让 PI 立刻看到你想清楚了。

## P10 · 合成、表征、测试与时间线

**英文讲稿**

## §14 两个必须在口播时说全称的歧义

1. **AEM**
   - P5 左图与 CSC-4 语境 = **adsorbate evolution mechanism**（吸附质演化机制），与 LOM 相对；
   - P7／P11 与 NC-1 语境 = **anion exchange membrane**（阴离子交换膜）电解槽。
   - 口播时说全称："the adsorbate evolution mechanism" vs "an anion-exchange-membrane electrolyser"。同一场报告里混用会让人以为在说同一件事。
2. **N_D = 0.37**（附表 NE-2 条目）
   - 它是 **RRDE 的收集效率（collection efficiency）**，用于把环电流换算成界面 pH；
   - **不是电子转移数，也不是选择性**。被问到就照这句答。
