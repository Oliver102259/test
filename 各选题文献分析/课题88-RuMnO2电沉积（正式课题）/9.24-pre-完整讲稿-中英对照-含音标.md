## P3 · 背景一：酸性水电解的瓶颈是耐久性

**英文讲稿**

> Let me start with why this project exists.
>
> PEM water electrolysis is the technology that couples well with intermittent renewables: it runs at high current density, it responds fast, and it delivers very pure hydrogen. The catch is the environment at the anode: roughly **one molar proton activity** — in other words, pH zero. In that environment, almost no transition-metal oxide survives. In practice, only iridium and ruthenium oxides do.
>
> And iridium is among the scarcest elements on earth. Even iridium oxide is not inert — at high potential it dissolves as the soluble complex **[IrO4]2-**. So the real constraint on the cost of a PEM stack is **not activity; it is durability at low noble-metal loading**. That is the problem my project sits inside.
>
> [指向右侧示意图] On the right is a schematic of the cell. The anode is the acidic, oxygen-evolving side — and that anode interface is exactly what I will be working on.

**中文对照**

> 先说这个课题为什么存在。
>
> PEM 水电解是与间歇性可再生能源匹配得最好的技术：高电流密度、响应快、产氢纯度很高。问题出在阳极环境：大约**1 M 的质子活度**——也就是 pH 0。在这种环境里，几乎没有任何过渡金属氧化物能存活，实际上只有铱和钌的氧化物可以。
>
> 而铱是地球上最稀缺的元素之一。而且即便是氧化铱也不是惰性的——在高电位下它会以可溶配合物 **[IrO4]2-** 的形式溶解。所以 PEM 电解槽成本的真正约束**不是活性，而是低贵金属载量下的耐久性**。我的课题就落在这个问题里。
>
> [指向右侧示意图] 右边是电解槽示意图。阳极就是酸性、析氧的那一侧——而那个阳极界面，正是我要做工作的地方。

## P4 · 背景二：Ru 的悖论——更活泼、更便宜，但更不稳定

**英文讲稿**

> Ruthenium was selected for two reasons. First, RuO2 is more active than IrO2 for the oxygen evolution reaction, and ruthenium is much cheaper and more abundant than iridium. Second, its limitation is stability, which is the property this project can change.
>
> Ruthenium fails through two channels. The first is over-oxidation: ruthenium is oxidised beyond +4 to soluble RuO4 and leaves the electrode. The second is lattice-oxygen participation: lattice oxygen is consumed by the reaction, and the metal is released with it.
>
> These channels are connected to a design trade-off, in which a higher O 2p band centre increases activity but decreases stability. The objective is therefore stabilisation rather than higher activity, and any stabilisation result requires a dissolution measurement, not only an overpotential.

**中文对照**

> 选择钌有两个原因。第一，在析氧反应中 RuO₂ 比 IrO₂ 更活泼，而钌比铱便宜得多、储量也丰富得多。第二，它的短板是稳定性，而这正是本课题可以改变的性质。
>
> 钌的失效有两条通道。第一条是过氧化：钌被氧化越过 +4 价，生成可溶的 RuO₄ 并离开电极。第二条是晶格氧参与：晶格氧被反应消耗，金属随之脱离。
>
> 这两条通道与一个设计权衡相关：O 2p 带中心越高，活性越高、稳定性越低。因此目标是稳定化，而不是提高活性；任何稳定化的结论都需要溶解测量支持，而不能只用过电位。

**台上提示**：指左框（两条通道）→ 指下方权衡框。两句结论放慢：`stabilisation rather than higher activity`、`requires a dissolution measurement`。

## P5 · 文献一：设计原则，以及为什么 MnO2 是被允许的

**英文讲稿**

> This slide gives the design rules from the review. The left figure shows the four steps of oxygen evolution. If all four steps had the same free energy, 1.23 electronvolts, the sum would be 4.92 electronvolts and the overpotential would be zero. On oxide surfaces this is not observed, because the intermediates *OH and *OOH are connected by a scaling relation and their free energies differ by about 3.2 electronvolts. The steps therefore become uneven. In this schematic the largest step is 1.60 electronvolts, which is 0.37 volts above 1.23 volts. This is the scaling limit for the adsorbate evolution mechanism.
>
> The right figure shows the volcano relation: activity is highest at an intermediate adsorption energy. The proton-acceptor route changes the mechanism. Nickel or cobalt in RuO2 activates a bridging oxygen and lowers the determining step to 1.49 or 1.33 electronvolts, that is 0.26 or 0.10 volts, below the 0.37 volt limit.
>
> Two further results support manganese. Gamma-MnO2 has been reported to operate for 8,000 hours at pH 2, and Mn3+ provides the optimal eg occupancy, eg equal to one. The same review also states that a suitable potential window must be identified.

**中文对照**

> 这一页是综述里的设计规则。左图是析氧的四个步骤。如果四步的自由能都等于 1.23 eV，总和为 4.92 eV，过电位为零。在氧化物表面观察不到这种情况，因为中间体 \*OH 与 \*OOH 之间存在标度关系，两者自由能相差约 3.2 eV。因此台阶并不均匀。本示意图中最大台阶为 1.60 eV，比 1.23 V 高 0.37 V。这就是吸附质演化机制的标度上限。
>
> 右图是火山关系：活性在中等吸附能处最高。质子受体路线改变了机理：RuO₂ 中的镍或钴活化桥氧，把决速步降到 1.49 或 1.33 eV，即 0.26 或 0.10 V，低于 0.37 V 的上限。
>
> 还有两条结果支持使用锰。γ-MnO₂ 被报道可在 pH 2 下运行 8,000 小时；Mn³⁺ 提供最优的 eg 占据数，eg 等于 1。同一篇综述也指出，必须先确定合适的电位窗口。

**台上提示**：左手点最大台阶 → 右手点火山顶 → 点 1.49/1.33 eV 那行注释。最后一句是过渡到 P9 的钩子。

## P6 · 文献二：重构化学，以及酸性中的 Ru 稳定化

**英文讲稿**

> The second paper concerns reconstruction. The film that is deposited is not the catalyst. During activation the surface converts to an oxyhydroxide, MOOH, and this is the active phase. The review describes four reconstruction mechanisms and six factors that control the activity of the reconstructed surface: exposed edge facets, residual dopant cations, unsaturated coordination and vacancies, adsorbed oxyanions, hybrid interfaces with electron transfer through bridging oxygen, and strain, which broadens the eg band.
>
> Two points are directly relevant to the plan. First, the activation protocol is an experimental variable: cyclic voltammetry gives complete oxidation, chronopotentiometry gives partial oxidation, and full reconstruction can take eight hours or more. Second, in acid, ruthenium fails through RuO4 formation and lattice-oxygen participation, and two stabilisation routes are established: confinement as single-atom sites, and an increase in the oxygen-vacancy formation energy. The review also records a precedent: ruthenium on alpha-MnO2 prepared by cation exchange, with an overpotential of 161 millivolts and a lifetime above 200 hours.

**中文对照**

> 第二篇文献讨论重构。沉积得到的膜不是催化剂。活化过程中表面转变为羟基氧化物 MOOH，这才是活性相。综述描述了四种重构机制，以及控制重构表面活性的六个因素：暴露的边缘晶面、残留的掺杂阳离子、不饱和配位与空位、吸附的含氧阴离子、通过桥氧传递电子的杂化界面，以及使 eg 带展宽的应变。
>
> 有两点与方案直接相关。第一，活化程序是实验变量：循环伏安给出完全氧化，恒电流给出部分氧化，完全重构可能需要八小时以上。第二，在酸性中钌通过生成 RuO₄ 与晶格氧参与失效，已有两类稳定化路线：限域为单原子位点，以及提高氧空位形成能。综述还记载了一个先例：通过阳离子交换制备的 α-MnO₂ 负载钌，过电位 161 mV，寿命超过 200 小时。

**台上提示**：第一句重读——`The film that is deposited is not the catalyst`。提到先例时必须说 `as recorded in our group's review`。

## P7 · 文献三：界面环境、掺杂实践、器件级验证

**英文讲稿**

> This slide gives one result from each of the remaining three papers.
>
> The first is that the local environment around the active site controls stability. In that work a hard-Lewis-acid layer keeps the local pH near 14 at 1.6 volts, which gives more than 200 hours in seawater with about 98 per cent oxygen selectivity. The local environment is therefore a design variable, and it differs from the bulk.
>
> The second is that doping stabilises the metal, and that the work is not complete until it is tested in a device. A chromium-doped amorphous oxide gives 268 millivolts at 10 milliamps per square centimetre, and the electrolyser runs for 120 hours with a decay below 4.9 millivolts per hour.
>
> The third is that a protecting unit can be redox-active: in that structure a variable-valence second metal buffers the oxidative charge.
>
> The gap is therefore not the chemistry. It is the construction of the interface: how to build Ru-O-Mn controllably and on a larger scale.

**中文对照**

> 这一页是其余三篇文献各一条结果。
>
> 第一条：活性位点周围的局部环境决定稳定性。那项工作用硬路易斯酸层把局部 pH 在 1.6 V 时保持在 14 附近，从而在海水中获得超过 200 小时寿命和约 98% 的氧气选择性。因此局部环境是一个设计变量，并且与本体不同。
>
> 第二条：掺杂可以稳定金属，而且工作必须做到器件验证才算完成。铬掺杂非晶氧化物在 10 mA cm⁻² 下过电位 268 mV，电解槽运行 120 小时，衰减低于 4.9 mV h⁻¹。
>
> 第三条：保护单元可以是变价的——在该结构中，变价第二金属缓冲氧化电荷。
>
> 因此缺口不在化学，而在界面的构建：如何可控且更大规模地构建 Ru–O–Mn。

## P8 · 缺口与假设：电沉积作为工艺杠杆

**英文讲稿**

> This slide states the hypothesis. Route A is a MnO2 shell around a RuO2 core. In this design the shell dissolves first, and the coating then fails. Route B places ruthenium inside the MnO2 framework so that the two share oxygen, forming Ru-O-Mn.
>
> The material hypothesis is that electrodeposited MnO2 acts as both support and redox mediator: the Mn3+/Mn4+ couple and the surface hydroxyl groups absorb oxidative charge and hold ruthenium near +4, outside the RuO4 window.
>
> The process hypothesis is that deposition parameters control the phase, the morphology and the density of edge sites, so that the density and configuration of the coupling can be scanned. The variables are the host deposition, at 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes, about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre, with a two-fold gradient; the current and the bath pH; and the route for ruthenium: co-deposition, impregnation or cation exchange. This process control is not available in the literature.

**中文对照**

> 这一页说明假设。Route A 是在 RuO₂ 核外包一层 MnO₂ 壳。在这种设计中壳先溶解，包覆层随后失效。Route B 把钌放进 MnO₂ 骨架内部，使两者共享氧，形成 Ru–O–Mn。
>
> 材料假设：电沉积 MnO₂ 同时作为载体与氧化还原介质——Mn³⁺/Mn⁴⁺ 电对与表面羟基吸收氧化电荷，使钌保持在 +4 附近，处于 RuO₄ 窗口之外。

## P9 · 主要风险，以及我们的 go / no-go 实验

**英文讲稿**

> The largest risk in this project is manganese, not ruthenium. In acid, MnO2 is stable only within a narrow potential range. Below about 1.4 volts against SHE, manganese dissolves as Mn2+. Between about 1.4 and 1.6 volts the dioxide is stable. Above about 1.6 volts permanganate can form, and it is soluble. Oxygen evolution requires more than 1.23 volts, so the overlap between the two ranges is small. This is the meaning of the review statement that a suitable potential window must be identified. If this window is narrow, or if it shifts under load, a MnO2 coating would dissolve before the ruthenium beneath it.
>
> The first experiment is therefore a decision test in weeks one to three, with four parts. First, a potential-step dissolution scan from 1.40 to 1.90 volts against RHE, with each step collected separately. Second, segmented ICP-MS, with ruthenium and manganese measured separately. Third, in-situ Raman, to detect the permanganate band near 839 reciprocal centimetres. Fourth, four controls: MnO2 only, RuO2, impregnated, and substituted.
>
> The decision rule is shown on the slide. If manganese dissolves much faster than ruthenium and cannot be suppressed, the MnO2 phase or the oxygen coordination will be changed, or ruthenium will be introduced first. Otherwise the work continues to interface engineering and the parameter sweep.

**中文对照**

> 本课题最大的风险是锰，不是钌。在酸性中，MnO₂ 只在一个狭窄的电位区间内稳定。低于约 1.4 V（vs SHE），锰以 Mn²⁺ 溶解；约 1.4 至 1.6 V 之间二氧化锰稳定；高于约 1.6 V 可能生成可溶的高锰酸根。析氧需要高于 1.23 V，因此两个区间的重叠很小。这就是综述所说"必须先确定合适的电位窗口"的含义。如果这个窗口很窄，或在负载下发生移动，MnO₂ 包覆层会在其下方的钌之前先溶解。
>
> 因此第一个实验是前三周的判定实验，包含四部分。第一，电位阶跃溶出扫描，从 1.40 到 1.90 V（vs RHE），每个电位段单独收集。第二，分段 ICP-MS，钌与锰分开测量。第三，原位拉曼，检测 839 cm⁻¹ 附近的高锰酸根谱带。第四，四个对照：只有 MnO₂、RuO₂、浸渍、取代。
>
> 判定规则在幻灯片上。如果锰的溶解明显快于钌且无法抑制，就改变 MnO₂ 的物相或氧配位，或者先引入钌；否则继续进入界面工程与参数扫描。

**台上提示**：`manganese, not ruthenium` 重读。左图三段电位用手沿横轴走。结尾补一句量化口径：同一电位段内 Mn 速率 ≥ Ru 的 5 倍且无钝化趋势，或出现 839 cm⁻¹ 谱带，即判 no-go。

## P10 · 合成、表征、测试与时间线

**英文讲稿**

> The plan has four parts.
>
> Synthesis. MnO2 is electrodeposited on titanium mesh or titanium felt. The host deposition is 7 milliamps per square centimetre for 15 minutes, about 6.3 coulombs per square centimetre, with a two-fold gradient. The current and the bath pH are variables. Ruthenium is introduced at a near-single-atom level, and one activation protocol is fixed for all samples.
>
> Characterisation. Paired XRD, Raman, XPS and HRTEM-EDS before and after activation; operando XAS at both the ruthenium and the manganese K-edges, which is the decisive measurement for Ru-O-Mn; and EPR for oxygen vacancies.
>
> Testing. iR-corrected LSV for the overpotential at 10 milliamps per square centimetre, the Tafel slope and EIS; normalisation by mass and by electrochemically active surface area; and long-term chronopotentiometry with segmented ICP-MS, reported with a stability number.
>
> Device. One single-cell test, reported as current density, duration and retention, with Faradaic efficiency.
>
> The milestones are 8 October 2026 for entry to the laboratory, 6 January 2027 for the mid-term review, and April 2027 for the final report.

**中文对照**

> 方案分四部分。
>
> 合成。MnO₂ 电沉积在钛网或钛毡上。宿主沉积为 7 mA cm⁻² 持续 15 分钟，约 6.3 C cm⁻²，并设两倍梯度；电流与浴 pH 为变量；钌以近单原子载量引入；所有样品固定同一套活化程序。
>
> 表征。活化前后配对做 XRD、拉曼、XPS 与 HRTEM-EDS；在钌与锰的 K 边同时做 operando XAS，这是判断 Ru–O–Mn 的决定性测量；用 EPR 测氧空位。
>
> 测试。iR 校正的 LSV 给出 10 mA cm⁻² 下的过电位、Tafel 斜率与 EIS；用质量与电化学活性面积双重归一化；长时恒电流配分段 ICP-MS，并给出稳定数。
>
> 器件。做一次单电池测试，按电流密度、时长与保持率报数，并给出法拉第效率。

## Q&A 预案（10 问，英文答句 + 中文）

| # | 可能的问题 | 英文答句（可直接念） |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Why Ru–O–Mn and not simply a MnO2 coating? | "Because a coating is only as good as its weakest component — the MnO2 shell would dissolve first. Putting Ru into the framework makes the two share oxygen, so the stabilising element cannot leave independently." |
| 2 | Is MnO2 really stable in acid? | "It has been reported for 8,000 hours at pH 2, but only within a suitable potential window — which is exactly why measuring that window is my first experiment." |
| 3 | How is this different from the Ru/alpha-MnO2 precedent? | "The precedent established the chemistry: cation exchange, oxide path mechanism, no oxygen vacancies, 161 millivolts, over 200 hours. | 4 | What if electrodeposition cannot put Ru into the lattice? | "Then the cation-exchange route is the fallback, and the substituted-versus-impregnated control in the go / no-go tells me which one I actually have." |
| 5 | What is your baseline? | "Two baselines: MnO2 only and RuO2 — so that any change can be attributed to the interface rather than to either component alone." |
| 6 | How will you prove that Ru is stabilised? | "With dissolution-based metrics, not overpotential: segmented ICP-MS for Ru and Mn, and a stability number — oxygen evolved per dissolved metal atom." |
| 7 | Why operando XAS? | "Because Ru–O–Mn coupling is a local-structure claim; the Ru and Mn K-edges measured in the same experiment are the direct test of it." |
| 8 | What is your biggest risk? | "Manganese dissolving before ruthenium. That is why the first three weeks are a binary go / no-go rather than an optimisation." |
| 9 | Timeframe — is three weeks realistic for the go / no-go? | "Yes, because it answers only one question: which element leaves first, and at which potential." |

## §14 两个必须在口播时说全称的歧义

1. **AEM**
   - P5 左图与 CSC-4 语境 = **adsorbate evolution mechanism**（吸附质演化机制），与 LOM 相对；
   - P7／P11 与 NC-1 语境 = **anion exchange membrane**（阴离子交换膜）电解槽。
   - 口播时说全称："the adsorbate evolution mechanism" vs "an anion-exchange-membrane electrolyser"。同一场报告里混用会让人以为在说同一件事。
2. **N_D = 0.37**（附表 NE-2 条目）
   - 它是 **RRDE 的收集效率（collection efficiency）**，用于把环电流换算成界面 pH；
   - **不是电子转移数，也不是选择性**。被问到就照这句答。
